You have the right to:

- Correct inaccurate data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@cosyarcade.com.

CosyArcade offers accounts for users under 13 with parental consent. We:

- Limit data collection from children

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

# Age Verification Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## Purpose

CosyArcade is committed to providing age-appropriate content to all users. This policy explains how we verify user ages and protect younger users.

## Content Ratings

### ALL (All Ages)
- Suitable for everyone
- No violence beyond cartoon/fantasy
- No mature themes
- No verification required

### TEEN_13 (Ages 13+)
- Mild cartoon violence
- Some competitive/challenging content
- Requires SMS verification to access

## Verification Methods

### For Users 13 and Over

**SMS Verification**
1. User provides mobile phone number
2. We send a 6-digit verification code via Twilio
3. User enters code within 10 minutes
4. Verification status is recorded (phone number is not stored)

**Why SMS?**
- Mobile phone ownership correlates with age
- Provides reasonable assurance without collecting sensitive documents
- Quick and user-friendly process

### For Users Under 13

**Parental Consent**
1. Child provides parent/guardian email address
2. We send verification email to parent
3. Parent clicks link to approve account creation
4. Child account is created with restricted access

**Parent Rights**
- Review what data we collect about their child
- Request deletion of their child's account
- Revoke consent at any time

## What Verification Does NOT Include

We do NOT:
- Request government ID or identity documents
- Collect date of birth (we verify age bracket, not exact age)
- Store phone numbers after verification
- Share verification data with third parties
- Use facial recognition or biometrics

## Bypassing Restrictions

Attempting to bypass age verification is a violation of our Terms of Service and may result in:

- Account suspension or termination
- IP-based access restrictions
- Reporting to relevant authorities if laws are broken

## For Parents

### How to Know if Your Child Has an Account

Child accounts require your email verification. If you receive a verification email you didn't expect:

1. Do not click the approval link
2. Contact us at parents@cosyarcade.com
3. We will investigate and take appropriate action

### Managing Your Child's Account

Parents can:
- View their child's play history
- Delete their child's account
- Modify content restrictions
- Receive usage reports (optional)

Contact parents@cosyarcade.com to manage your child's account.

## Compliance

This policy is designed to comply with:

- Australian Privacy Act 1988
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) principles
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles

## Updates

We may update this policy as regulations change or our service evolves. Significant changes will be announced on our website.

## Contact

For questions about age verification:

- Parents: parents@cosyarcade.com
- General: support@cosyarcade.com

# Content Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## Our Commitment

CosyArcade is committed to providing a curated library of quality retro games that are:

1. **Legally free** to distribute
2. **Appropriately rated** for our audience
3. **Functional** in our browser-based emulator
4. **Quality** experiences worth playing

## Legal Requirements

### What We Host

- **Homebrew**: Original games created for retro hardware
- **Freeware**: Games officially released for free by copyright holders
- **Open Source**: Games with open source licences
- **Public Domain**: Games with expired or released copyrights

### What We Never Host

- Pirated commercial games
- Games with unclear copyright status
- ROMs extracted from commercial cartridges
- Games without documented legal source

### Documentation

Every game in our library includes:

- Source URL (where we obtained it)
- Licence type (homebrew, freeware, open source, etc.)
- Author/publisher attribution
- Any distribution terms

## Content Standards

### Prohibited Content

We do not host games containing:

- Real-world violence promotion
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexual content or nudity
- Illegal activities promotion
- Gambling with real money

### Age Rating Guidelines

**ALL (All Ages)**
- Cartoon/fantasy violence only (if any)
- No blood or gore
- No mature themes
- Positive or neutral messaging

**TEEN_13 (Ages 13+)**
- Mild cartoon violence
- Fantasy combat
- Challenging/competitive content
- No realistic violence
- No sexual content

## Curation Process

### New Game Submissions

1. **Source Verification**: Confirm legal status
2. **Technical Testing**: Verify works in EmulatorJS
3. **Content Review**: Play through to assess content
4. **Age Rating**: Assign appropriate rating
5. **Documentation**: Record all metadata
6. **Publication**: Add to library

### Community Reports

Users can report games for:

- Copyright concerns
- Incorrect age rating
- Technical issues
- Content concerns

Reports are reviewed within 48 hours.

## Removal Policy

Games may be removed if:

- Copyright holder requests removal
- Legal status becomes unclear
- Content no longer meets our standards
- Technical issues cannot be resolved

## Disclaimer

While we make every effort to verify legal status:

- We rely on publicly available information
- Copyright law varies by jurisdiction
- We will promptly respond to legitimate takedown requests

## DMCA / Takedown Requests

If you believe your copyrighted work is hosted on CosyArcade without authorisation:

1. Email dmca@cosyarcade.com with:
   - Your contact information
   - Description of the copyrighted work
   - URL of the infringing content
   - Statement of good faith belief
   - Statement of accuracy under penalty of perjury
   - Your physical or electronic signature

2. We will respond within 48 hours
3. Infringing content will be removed promptly

## Contact

- Content questions: content@cosyarcade.com
- Copyright concerns: dmca@cosyarcade.com
- General: support@cosyarcade.com
