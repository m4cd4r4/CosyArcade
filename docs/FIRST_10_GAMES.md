# First 10 Games for CosyArcade

This document outlines the first 10 games to launch CosyArcade, with **verified legal status**.

---

## Selection Criteria

1. **Legally verifiable** - Clear homebrew/freeware status with source
2. **Quality** - Actually fun to play
3. **Platform diversity** - Spread across different consoles
4. **Lower legal risk** - Prioritise non-Nintendo platforms initially
5. **Age appropriate** - Suitable for all ages where possible
