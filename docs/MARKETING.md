## Brand Voice

### Personality
- **Warm**: Like a cosy blanket and a cup of tea
- **Nostalgic**: Celebrating the joy of retro gaming
- **Welcoming**: Inclusive, family-friendly, accessible
- **Honest**: Legal games only, no tricks or dark patterns
- **Relaxed**: No pressure, no competition, just play

### Tone
- Friendly, not corporate
- Enthusiastic, not hype-y
- Knowledgeable, not gatekeeping
- Cosy, not childish

### Words We Use
- Cosy, warm, comfortable, relaxing
- Retro, classic, vintage, nostalgic
- Play, enjoy, discover, remember
- Free, legal, curated, quality
- Home, corner, haven, sanctuary

### Words We Avoid
- Extreme, epic, insane, crazy
- Grind, hustle, compete
- Download, install, register
- Ads, premium, paywall
- ROM, emulation (in user-facing copy)

## Feature Descriptions

### For Landing Page

**Browser-Based**
> No downloads, no installs, no waiting. Click a game and start playing instantly in your browser. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

**100% Legal**
> Every game on CosyArcade is legally free — homebrew, freeware, or open source. We document the source of every game. No piracy, ever.

**Save Your Progress**
> Create a free account to save your game states to the cloud. Pick up exactly where you left off, on any device.

**Family Friendly**
> Age-appropriate content filtering keeps younger players safe. Child accounts require parental consent. Teen content requires verification.

**Four Ambient Themes**
> Set the mood with CRT Glow, Afternoon Sunlight, Lamp-lit Evening, or Rainy Day. Each theme has its own colour palette and atmosphere.

**Mobile Ready**
> Touch controls for handheld games make CosyArcade perfect for gaming on the go. Your progress syncs across all your devices.

### Medium (for Platform Pages)

**Arcade**
> The golden age of gaming! Classic cabinet games that defined generations. From maze games to shooters, these are the games that started it all.

**NES / Nintendo Entertainment System**
> The console that saved gaming. The NES brought arcade-quality games into living rooms worldwide. Home to some of the most iconic games ever made.

**SNES / Super Nintendo**
> 16-bit perfection. The SNES elevated gaming with better graphics, better sound, and deeper gameplay. A golden era for platformers and RPGs.

**Sega Genesis / Mega Drive**
> Sega does what Nintendon't! The Genesis brought attitude and blast processing to gaming. Home to fast-paced action and sports games.

**Game Boy**
> Gaming on the go. Nintendo's legendary handheld let you take your games anywhere. Simple graphics, unforgettable gameplay.

**Game Boy Advance**
> The ultimate handheld. The GBA packed SNES-quality gaming into your pocket. Backwards compatible with all Game Boy games.

**Commodore 64**
> The best-selling computer ever. The C64 introduced a generation to gaming and programming. Home to thousands of classic games.

**Amiga**
> Ahead of its time. The Amiga's multimedia capabilities made it a gaming powerhouse. Stunning graphics and sound for the era.

## Theme Descriptions

### Short (for Theme Selector)

| ☀️ Afternoon Sunlight | After school vibes | Warm golden |

### Long (for Settings/About)

**🌙 CRT Glow**
> It's late. The house is quiet. Your face is lit only by the soft glow of the screen. This is CRT Glow — our default theme for night owls and dedicated gamers. Cool purples and soft phosphor glows create the perfect late-night atmosphere.

**☀️ Afternoon Sunlight**
> School's out. The afternoon sun streams through the curtains. You've got a few hours before dinner. Afternoon Sunlight brings warm, golden tones that capture that "after school gaming" feeling.

**💡 Lamp-lit Evening**
> Settled into the couch, lamp glowing nearby. Maybe it's raining outside. Lamp-lit Evening wraps you in warm amber and cosy orange tones — perfect for a relaxed gaming session.

**🌧️ Rainy Day**
> There's something special about gaming when it's grey outside. The patter of rain, a warm drink nearby, nowhere to be. Rainy Day's cool blue-greys create the perfect indoor-cosy atmosphere.
